One of our technicians

Welcome to our website. Thank you for visiting Bluevane Heating & Cooling, Inc. We are a full service heating and air conditioning company proudly serving the Greater Pittsburgh area and surrounding communities since 1998. We understand that your home comfort is important all year round and that is why we offer a complete range of quality heating and cooling services for both residential and light commercial customers at affordable prices.

From the coldest days of winter to the hottest days of summer you can count on Bluevane to keep your systems running at peak performance. Our experienced technicians are trained on the latest equipment and technology. We service all makes and models and we stand behind all of our work. When you need comfort, quality and value, call Bluevane Heating & Cooling, Inc. today. We look forward to hearing from you soon!

Our Services Include:

  • AC repair
  • Furnace repair
  • System installation
  • Seasonal maintenance
  • 24 hour emergency service
